# ArtCraft vertical

## Why

The owner wants art and craft businesses (eternal sunflowers, gifts, decor) that take custom orders with reference
photos, materials, deposit and delivery date. Orders and stock exist in BusinessKit; artisans need works with
options, costing from materials and a production view.

## What changes

- New module ArtCraftModule (port 50075) composing core + retail + orders.
- Works with options and occasions, bill of materials, cost estimates, custom orders with stored specs, material
  consumption and a production board.
